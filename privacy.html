2. In short

  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking tools.

I share personal data only with the services the Game runs on, and never for advertising. I do not sell or rent your data.

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Economy, Leaderboards) hosts the online features and processes data on my behalf under Unity's data processing terms. See the Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Google Play, Google Play Billing, Google Play Console) handles downloads, payments, refunds and reviews as an independent controller under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Other players see your nickname, score and rank on leaderboards.
  • My email provider, [Email provider], stores the emails you send me.
  • Authorities or courts, only when the law requires it or when needed to establish or defend legal claims.
  • A new owner of the Game, if I transfer the Game, for example to my own company. The new owner must follow this policy or tell you about any change in advance.

7. Deleting your data

The Game has no delete button, so deletion works by email and is free.

  1. Email [Contact email] with the subject "Delete my Garden data".
  2. Include your Player ID ([Where to find the Player ID]) or your full nickname with the number after the # sign, for example RoseLover#1234.
  3. If you made purchases, add the order number from your Google Play receipt so I can confirm the account is yours.
  4. I delete your account, progress, currency, items, nickname and leaderboard entries within one month and confirm by email.

Deletion is final: progress and unused purchased currency cannot be restored. Uninstalling the Game does not delete data on the server; it only removes the copy on your phone. Purchase records required by law are kept as described in section 6.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted (section 7);
  • restrict how your data is processed;
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format and move it elsewhere;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • complain to a data protection authority.

Email [Contact email] to use any of these rights. I reply within one month; for complex requests this can be extended by two more months, and I will tell you why. Requests are free.

In Latvia the supervisory authority is the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija). You can also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

9. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 14 and older. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 14; if you believe a child under 14 has used the Game's online features, email me and I will delete the data. Players under 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission to make purchases. Please do not use your real name or other personal details as a nickname.

10. Security

The Game connects to Unity services over encrypted connections, and data is stored on Unity's secured infrastructure. Only I can access the Unity and Google Play developer accounts, which are protected with two-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure; if a data breach puts your rights at high risk, I will inform you and the supervisory authority as the GDPR requires.
